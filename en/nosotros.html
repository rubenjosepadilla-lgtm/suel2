About

suel2 is modern payroll software for Chile and Latin America. It automates payslips, AFP contributions, Previred, SII and salary payments, with 100% up-to-date legal compliance.

Why suel2 exists

Companies lose days every month calculating salaries in Excel, fixing errors and chasing legal changes (minimum wage, AFP tables, caps). A fine or a wrong calculation is expensive. We built suel2 so payroll stops being a pain: simple, up to date and error-free.

Who it's for

  • Companies of any size: that want to pay salaries correctly and on time, with compliance up to date and no spreadsheets.
  • Accountants: managing payroll for several companies from a single dashboard.
  • ERP partners: who need to close the Chilean payroll gap their ERP doesn't cover.

Who's behind it

suel2 is an initiative by Sapport, a Chilean technology company with years of experience supporting organizations in their software and people projects. We built suel2 with a simple idea: make world-class payroll accessible to companies of every scale —from those just starting out to those operating in several countries.

How we work

We speak plainly and with data, not promises. We prefer simple over complicated, and real compliance over "always up to date" as a slogan. Made in Chile, built for the whole region.
