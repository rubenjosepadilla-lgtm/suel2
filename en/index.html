Frequently asked questions

What is suel2?

suel2 is payroll and compensation software for Chile that automates payslip calculation, AFP and health contributions, income tax and legal bonuses, with Previred and SII integration and 100% up-to-date Chilean labor-law compliance. It is an initiative by Sapport.

Does suel2 comply with Chilean labor law?

Yes. suel2 keeps social security indicators, the monthly minimum wage, taxable caps, AFP tables and the income-tax table up to date according to the official Chilean bodies (SII, Superintendency of Pensions and the Labor Directorate).

Does suel2 integrate with Previred and the SII?

Yes. suel2 includes integration with Previred for social security contributions and with the SII for fee receipts and filings, all from the same platform.

Is it useful for accountants managing several companies?

Yes. suel2 lets you manage payroll for all of your client companies from a single dashboard, with per-company access levels and centralized reporting.

How do I get started with suel2?

Write to us at hola@suel2.com and we'll discuss how suel2 can help your company or accounting practice. We support you through onboarding.
