Frequently asked questions

Can I manage several companies from one account?

Yes. suel2 lets you manage payroll for all your client companies from a single dashboard, with per-company access and reporting.

How does the accountant commission work?

You earn a recurring monthly commission for every company you add to suel2, for as long as that company stays active on the platform.

Does suel2 calculate Previred and SII automatically?

Yes, with native integration: social security contributions in Previred and receipts/filings in the SII, all from the platform.

How much does it cost my practice?

The plan depends on the number of companies and employees you manage. Write to us and we'll work it out with you, with preferential terms for founding accountants.
