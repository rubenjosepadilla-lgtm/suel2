Frequently asked questions

How do you go from net to gross salary?

By adding back the deductions (AFP, health, unemployment insurance and tax) on top of the net. Since they depend on the taxable amount, it's solved in reverse: finding the gross whose net matches the one you want.

Is health always 7%?

The legal minimum is 7% (Fonasa). With an Isapre the plan is set in UF and can exceed 7%; there the deduction is the plan value.

What changes with a fixed-term contract?

The unemployment insurance: in an indefinite contract the worker contributes 0.6% and the employer 2.4%; in a fixed-term contract the worker contributes nothing and the employer pays 3.0%.

What are employer contributions?

Costs the employer pays on top of the salary: their share of unemployment insurance, SIS and mutual. Added to gross, they give the total cost to the company.
