Frequently asked questions

What does a severance (finiquito) include in Chile?

It can include unused proportional vacation, the indemnity for years of service (only in dismissal for company needs, art. 161), the indemnity in lieu of notice (if 30 days' notice was not given) and the pending proportional legal bonus, plus the wages of the last period.

When does the indemnity for years of service apply?

When the dismissal is for company needs (art. 161) and the contract was in force for one year or more. It equals 30 days of the last salary per year of service and any fraction over six months, capped at 11 years and 90 UF of salary.

How is proportional vacation calculated?

Based on time worked since the last contract anniversary: 15 business days of annual leave per year equal 1.25 business days per month, paid according to the worker's daily wage.

Is there a finiquito for voluntary resignation?

Yes, but without indemnity for years of service or notice. Proportional vacation, the proportional bonus and pending wages apply.
